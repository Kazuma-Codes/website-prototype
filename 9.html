01 / About the Hackathon

Where Ideas Meet Innovation

◆

Code Yudh – Battle of Codes is a software development hackathon organized by the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, Chandigarh University.

The hackathon challenges participants to identify real-world problems, develop innovative technology-driven solutions, and transform their ideas into functional prototypes.

The event follows a hybrid format — beginning with an online PPT screening round and culminating in an intense 24-hour offline hackathon at Chandigarh University, where teams collaborate, build, test, refine and present alongside mentors, industry professionals and faculty.

07 / Evaluation

How Champions Are Measured

Phase 1 — PPT Screening

Online round criteria
Innovation I

How innovative is the proposed solution?

Feasibility II

Can the proposed solution realistically be developed and implemented?

Technical Approach III

How effectively does the proposed technology approach address the problem?

Potential Impact IV

What potential impact can the proposed solution create?

Phase 2 — Final Hackathon

Offline finale criteria
Innovation I

The originality and innovative nature of the solution.

Implementation II

How effectively the proposed solution has been developed.

Functionality III

The functionality and demonstration of the developed prototype.

Impact IV

The potential impact of the final solution.
